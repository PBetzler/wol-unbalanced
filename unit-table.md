# WoL CCM Mod — Unit & Upgrade Planning Table

**Status: DRAFT — fill in the `Comments` column with hints / desired changes, then hand the file back.**

How to read this file:

- **Cost / Supply / Build time** are WoL *campaign* values (seconds at Normal speed), cross-checked against Liquipedia campaign pages. A few campaign values differ from multiplayer (e.g. Ghost 150/150, Reaper 40s). All numbers get re-verified against the actual game data (XML catalogs) during implementation, so don't worry about correcting small stat errors here.
- **Campaign Armory** = the campaign armory upgrades bought with credits between missions on the Hyperion (cost in parentheses, e.g. 50k = 50,000 credits) — *not* the in-game Armory building. Per rule 2 these will all be **auto-unlocked** in our mod.
- **Archipelago (AP)** = upgrades the SC2 Archipelago mod can grant this unit (names only — effects in Appendix A). For progressive items only the highest level is listed.
- **WOLRE** = Wings of Liberty Perpetual Randomizer. Its upgrades are not per-unit: every unit belongs to a **category** (Basic / Assault / Spellcasters / Stealth / Transport / Capital / Defensive) and rolls random upgrades from that category's pool. The WOLRE column names the unit's category — full pools in Appendix B. Categories marked \* (Support, Siege) have no pool column in the mod's spreadsheet, see Appendix B. Source: the mod's official spreadsheet, downloaded to `mods/perpetual-randomizer.xlsx`.

## Project rules (from the brief)

1. No new units beyond the base game (exceptions must be named explicitly).
2. All armory upgrades auto-unlocked as soon as their unit is unlocked.
3. All mercenaries unlocked together with their regular counterpart (in the unit's unlock mission).
4. Mercenaries keep their percentage advantage over the regular unit; all upgrades for regular units also apply to mercs.
5. No unit takes longer than 60 seconds to build.
6. Mercenary calldowns: unlimited charges, available from game start.
7. Attack windup (damage point) of all player Terran units capped at 0.1 s.
8. Any unit with a cloak ability cloaks for free — no energy cost, no drain, no regen hindrance.
9. All stat/upgrade changes apply ONLY to the player's units — enemies keep vanilla values ("my units unbalanced and fun, not theirs").
10. Hero units inherit all changes of their base unit (like mercenaries): Raynor = Marine, Tychus = Firebat, Swann = Marauder, Stetmann = Medic; inferred additionally: Nova = Ghost, Tosh = Spectre, Odin = Thor.

## Appendix B — WOLRE (Perpetual Randomizer) upgrade pools

Source: the mod's official spreadsheet (downloaded to `mods/perpetual-randomizer.xlsx`); the mod itself (maps + `CCMRandomizerWOL.SC2Mod`, `EZupgrade.SC2Mod`, `CampaignRandomizerBaseDependency.SC2Mod`) is in `mods/Perpetual Randomizer/` for data extraction during implementation.

How it works: every unit slot is randomized into a replacement unit; each unit has a **category**, and its random upgrades are drawn from that category's pool:


| Pool                       | Upgrades                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Basic**                  | +25% max life · +25% move speed · +25% attack speed · +1 weapon range · −50% build time · spawn broodlings on death (= supply) · refund 20% cost on death                                                                                           |
| **Assault**                | +25% max life · +25% dmg vs armored · +25% dmg vs light + attacks ignite · no Tech Lab required · +20% max shields · +2 armor · +1 range · attacks pillage resources from buildings · attacks ignore armor                                         |
| **Spellcasters**           | +50 max energy · +50 starting energy · +25% energy regen · +2 life regen · +1 armor aura · +0.5 life regen aura (stacks ×5) · +25% move speed aura · costs 25% less vespene                                                                        |
| **Stealth**                | +3 vision & +2 weapon range · 20% dodge chance (listed twice in sheet) · +50% move speed out of combat · +25% dmg while undetected · +50% energy regen out of combat · Emergency Recall ability · +50% dmg vs massive · attacks drain target energy |
| **Transport**              | +40% max life · +30% max shields · slowly heal loaded cargo · +25% move speed · +8 cargo slots · unloaded units get +25% attack/move speed for 10 s                                                                                                   |
| **Capital**                | +30% max life · +25% max shields · +1 range · −20% cost · +25% damage · auto-heal up to 50% life · −33% supply · +25% attack speed · +25% move speed                                                                                             |
| **Defensive** (structures) | +25% max life · +25% attack speed · salvage deals 100 dmg nearby · +25% damage · +1 range · affected by vehicle upgrades · spawn 6 broodlings on death                                                                                               |

\* **Support** (Medic, Medivac, Raven, Science Vessel, Hercules) and **Siege** (Siege Tank) categories appear in the unit list but have **no pool column** in the spreadsheet — presumably they draw from Spellcasters/Transport/Assault respectively, or have undocumented pools. Can be verified from the mod data files if it matters.

Data notes: WOLRE's unit list gives Ghost 200/100 while Liquipedia's campaign page says 150/150 — to be settled from the game XML during implementation. Mercenaries are not separately categorized in WOLRE.
